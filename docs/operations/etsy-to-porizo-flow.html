Etsy → Porizo → Etsy

A made-to-order song starts and ends on Etsy. Porizo is the internal production queue. It never becomes the buyer's delivery channel.

The hard boundary. Only Etsy sends the buyer the final download link. Porizo records the operator's completion attestation after the Etsy upload.
1

Buyer buys and personalizes on Etsy

The buyer enters recipient name, occasion, memory, relationship, and style at Etsy checkout.

Etsy + buyer
2

Operator verifies the paid Etsy unit

In Shop Manager, confirm the shop, listing, receipt, transaction, quantity ordinal, and paid status. Then record the restricted evidence reference.

Operator
3

Operator creates the MTO unit in Porizo

Porizo stores the Etsy unit identity, creates an AI-voice track and version, generates lyrics, and waits for lyric approval.

Porizo
4

Approve lyrics and render

Approval starts a zero-wallet full render. The MTO marker prevents Porizo share tokens, buyer emails, and public play links.

Porizo + operator
5

Verify and download the MP3

Porizo promotes the unit only after a verified full MP3 exists with a storage key, SHA-256 hash, and Etsy-safe size.

Porizo + operator
6

Upload and complete the exact Etsy order

The operator opens Shop Manager, uploads the downloaded MP3, and completes the order. This remains a manual Etsy action.

Operator + Etsy
7

Record the Etsy completion attestation

The operator retypes the receipt and records restricted completion evidence. Porizo pins the artifact and version that were uploaded.

Porizo + operator
8

Etsy gives the buyer the download

Etsy emails its native download link to the buyer. The paid buyer-account test must prove this final step before launch.

What Porizo does not do. It does not issue an Etsy redemption code, consume a buyer credit, send the buyer an email, create a public share URL, upload to Etsy by API, or claim that an attestation proves Etsy delivery.
