# Cold Outreach Email Sequence — D2C Introduction

**Target:** 4,500 cold contacts (existing email list)
**Goal:** Introduce Porizo app, drive App Store downloads
**Sequence:** 3 emails over 8 days
**Format:** Plain text (no HTML/images — better deliverability for cold email)
**Tool:** Apollo.io free tier (250/day, ~18 days to clear list)

---

**Subject line options (A/B test):**
- A: `What if your favorite memory became a song?`
- B: `The gift no one expects (but everyone loves)`

---

## Notes

- **Plain text only** — no HTML, no images. 2-3x better deliverability for cold email.
- **"Re:" subject on email 2** — increases open rates ~22% (looks like a reply thread).
- **P.S. with unsubscribe on email 3 only** — CAN-SPAM compliance without cluttering early emails.
- **Sample link in email 1** — hearing is believing for a music product. Replace with actual sample URL.
- **"Someone's birthday is coming up" in email 3** — triggers the reader to think of someone specific.
- **Under 120 words per email** — cold emails over 150 words get skipped.
